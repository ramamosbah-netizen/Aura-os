## Architecture and business logic

The reusable clause library remains the source authority. A contract revision is
the contract-specific snapshot boundary: it stores structured terms and copied
clause snapshots, carries parent lineage, and records actor/time/reason. The
ContractService remains the lifecycle authority for the contract itself;
ContractRevisionService owns only pre-sign content revisions. Signed revisions
are immutable. Post-sign amendments/variations remain with C6 and are not
duplicated here.
