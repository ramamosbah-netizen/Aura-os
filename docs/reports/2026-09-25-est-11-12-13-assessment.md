# EST-11, EST-12, EST-13 — independent assessment against the frozen definition

2026-09-25. Assessed after the pre-award sourcing work (stages A–E: `bd84d966`, `bc7f446b`, `330dd680`,
`510776b8`) and the stage F screens, **measured** against the running API with auth on, a PostgreSQL
database rebuilt from zero (387 migrations, `e2e-disposable`), and the shipped roles in a browser.

**Result: EST-11 and EST-13 meet every layer of the frozen definition. EST-12 meets every layer but one,
which is a question only the programme owner can answer, so it is not promoted.** EST-16 is unchanged.

## The definition each is held to

All three carry the same frozen acceptance text:

> Representative Pre-Sales / Estimator, Commercial Manager / QS, Technical Manager role executes *X* in
> the canonical Estimation core / Tender estimate / CRM pricing context; save/reload, applicable
> permission denials, actual output and next-role receipt are proved.

…and the programme owner's instructions for this vertical: the real three-supplier path is primary and
the waiver is the exception; the counting criteria (independent suppliers, a valid revision, lines linked
through requisition lines and the material master, a saved technical decision per line, a governed
commercial comparison, the **required scope covered**); and *do not promote because three attachments
exist*. Nothing here counts attachments: a typed VENDOR_QUOTE reference on a tender offer is refused 409.

| | The act, in the tender-estimate context | Whose authority it reuses |
|---|---|---|
| **EST-11** Supplier quotation source | The estimate's material supply price is taken from ONE governed supplier quotation line — revision, line, requirement, material, currency, verdict, comparison date — never a quote header. | Estimation, over Procurement's quotation lines |
| **EST-12** Technical comparison | The Technical Manager's verdict on each supplier line decides which offers may price the bid and which count towards coverage. | SUP-13 technical evaluation (ADR-0022) |
| **EST-13** Commercial comparison | The governed comparison normalises every supplier line on a stated date; the estimate takes that figure, and coverage counts only comparable, live offers. | SUP-06 commercial comparison |

## The proof

`apps/web/e2e/tender-real-supply-path.spec.ts`, green, shipped users signed in through the login form:

1. **Estimator** maps each BOQ item to a same-unit material and puts the scope to suppliers on screen;
   the pricing requisition is read back — `tender_pricing`, `draft`, each line mapped by `u-e2e-estimator`.
2. Procurement quotes three suppliers (seeded — SUP-06/SUP-13 surfaces are proved on their own).
3. **Technical Manager**, on the evaluator's own screen, judges Al Noor's camera *Not compliant* and its
   cable *Compliant*; read back under the Buyer's permission (`not_eligible` / `eligible`, decided by
   `u-e2e-techmgr`).
4. **Estimator** reads coverage: *Supply scope NOT covered — 1 of 2*; camera *2 of 3 count*, Al Noor
   excluded "judged non-compliant by the Technical Manager"; cable *3 of 3*. Opens the comparison; the
   **comparison sheet PDF** names all three suppliers. Takes the supply price from Gulf Security's
   camera line (395 AED) and Emirates ELV's cable line (5.9 AED); both survive a **page reload**; the
   sources read back governed, with verdict and line.
5. **Denials:** the estimator recording a verdict 403; the Technical Manager pricing the bid 403; the
   Sales Manager reading the supplier comparison 403.
6. **Commercial Manager (independent approver)** follows the approvals inbox to the offer's Approval
   tab: *Vendor quotes — counted from the tender's supplier quotations — 1 of 2 items covered*; Approve
   is refused (*VENDOR_QUOTE (1/2)*). A fourth supplier confirms a compliant camera; the tab reads
   *2 of 2 items covered*; approval succeeds.
7. The approval **records the evidence it was decided on**: VENDOR_QUOTE `PROVIDED`, one reference per
   supply item naming each counted supplier's revision and line — Techno Secure on the camera, Al Noor
   only on the cable. Baseline locked by `u-e2e-qs2`.
8. **Sales Manager** fetches the offer PDF and the technical proposal PDF, and records the submission
   through the new form; read back: portal, reference, `submittedValue` equal to the locked baseline,
   submitted by `u-e2e-salesmgr`.

Also measured: the 36-case SQL suite for migration 0387 (including 10 new estimate-source cases: one
lineage only, governed lineage complete in each of six fields); the internal pricing workbook's
*Supplier Sources* sheet read back from the live tender with each governed source's revision, line,
requisition line, verdict, comparison date and currency; the full monorepo `test typecheck lint` (78/78).
